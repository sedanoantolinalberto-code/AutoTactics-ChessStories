## Direct original-Swiss-journal archive candidate
The Schweizerische Vereinigung der Kunstschachfreunde maintains a year-by-year digitization catalogue of `Schweizerische Schachzeitung` (Swiss Chess Journal), explaining scanned historical holdings:
https://www.kunstschach.ch/ssz.html

Search indexing reveals original-looking annual PDF `SSZ 1937.pdf`:
https://www.kunstschach.ch/docs/ssz/SSZ%201937.pdf

However direct open redirects to a Cloudflare verification page (“Please wait while your request is being verified”). Thus its search excerpts are **not** a visually inspected original print page. The 1937 full journal year is **an identified archival target**, not independently transcribed proof of any Euwe–Alekhine quotation. Verify month/issue/printed page and check whether the `Schweizerische` journal has the identical text quoted by Europe Échecs.

## Additional new 1937 cross-national context
New Zealand's National Library Papers Past gives an original newspaper column dated **11 Dec 1937**, reporting contemporary commentary on the championship and game one:
https://paperspast.natlib.govt.nz/newspapers/ESD19371211.2.24

This is an original periodical online transcription, **not yet a scanned page visually inspected**, and may quote another chess magazine; do not count it as independent match eyewitness testimony. It helps establish international journalistic dissemination and original press/review-lineage investigation, but its statements about player health or alcohol consumption are unattributed historical press claims, not established clinical fact.
