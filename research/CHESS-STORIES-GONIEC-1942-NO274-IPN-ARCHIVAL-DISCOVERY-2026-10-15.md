# Chess Stories — Corrected Polish primary source: Goniec Krakowski 1942 no. 274
Date: 2026-10-15. Documentary research, chess pilot only.

## Material new archival evidence
**Primary institution's collection INVENTORY (directly read), not yet the original newspaper page:** Instytut Pamięci Narodowej (IPN), branch archive Rzeszów, reference **IPN Rz 616/2**, collection *Mieczysław Krasnopolski*, lists individually dated editions of the occupation-era Polish newspaper *Goniec Krakowski*. The inventory explicitly pairs **nr 274** with **22/23-11-1942**, **nr 271** with **19-11-1942**, **nr 277** with **26-11-1942**, and **nr 280** with **29/30-11-1942**.

Canonical inventory: https://inwentarz.ipn.gov.pl/node/2583114?language_content_entity=pl (description, lines 38–44 in the retrieved version).

**Critical correction:** Prior candidate **nr 273**, Jagiellonian publication 24390 / edition 18937, was never day-dated by the visible bibliographic record. The official IPN list now directly supports **nr 274** (dated 22/23 November 1942) as the highest priority. **DO NOT claim issue 273 = 22 November.** The original clipping shown by Edward Winter and credited to 22 November p3 must be matched against nr274; a dual-dated newspaper may label a section/date differently. Page 3 of nr274 has **not** been visually inspected. Inventory metadata is not the same as image of original page.

## Other 1942 corroboration
IPN inventory also records nr263 10 Nov, nr264 11 Nov, nr266 13 Nov, nr271 19 Nov, nr274 22/23 Nov, nr277 26 Nov, nr278 27 Nov, nr279 28 Nov, nr280 29/30 Nov, nr281 1 Dec. These give dated anchors to check any candidate, but do not identify the contents of page 3.

Edward Winter: https://www.chesshistory.com/winter/extra/byrnefischer.html attributes the Russner–Walcker report to *Goniec Krakowski*, 22 November 1942 p3; the Winter-reproduced historic clipping is partial.

Jagiellonian group https://jbc.bj.uj.edu.pl/dlibra/publication/19185/goniec-krakowski-1942 has digital ID DIGCZAS001749, shelfmark 409317 IV. Attempt to find a stable indexed digital entry for **nr274** yielded none in current search, but non-indexing does not demonstrate unavailability from the full Jagiellonian viewer.
