## Question
Do later “mate over the board” and “resigned” narratives supply independent evidence of how Byrne–Fischer 1956 ended? **Not demonstrated.** Separate the printed score, mathematical mating position, alleged action on board, and formal event/result reporting.

They may share a common score source. Two publications do **not** imply two independent on-site eyewitness accounts.

## Non-substitutability of claims
- **MATE_AS_PRINTED** = printed notation calls a terminal position mate. Observed at limited contemporaneous levels above.
- **MATE_AS_LEGAL_POSITION** = independently verified chess legality/checkmate; chess oracle **NOT_RUN**, so not validated by this project.
- **MATE_PLAYED_ON_BOARD** = historical physical move occurred; contemporaneous score does not conclusively establish this.
- **FORMAL_TERMINATION_MODE** = tournament/arbiter classification as resignation/checkmate; not independently established.
- **GAME_SCORE_0_1** = result shown in modern references, distinct from formal termination mode.
- **PUBLIC_EP01_T12** = deliberately ends at ply 50, not terminal; never backfill mate from any terminal claim.

## Evidential decision
The fact of conflicting retrospective claims is evidence about **historiography**, not evidence that one scenario happened. A modern article that contradicts the played move `18.Bxb6` cannot be used to establish terminal details. Multiple modern versions may rest on the same retellings or scoresheets; **independent witness count stays unassessed**. No probability of actual resignation versus mate is assigned.

## Specific upgrading evidence
A verified game scoresheet *with documented custody*, contemporaneous participant/arbiter narrative explicitly distinguishing resignation from played mate, or archival tournament result/incident sheet would materially advance this question. Even a signed score noting `41...Rc2#` needs separate interpretation regarding actual delivery vs pencilled/analysed notation. The Chess Review original page is still needed for direct textual image validation; independent blind readers of Chess Life remain NOT_RUN.
