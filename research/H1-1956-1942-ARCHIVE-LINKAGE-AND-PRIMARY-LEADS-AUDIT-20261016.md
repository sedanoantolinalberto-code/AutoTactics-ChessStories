## A. New high-value bibliographic controls
### *Chess Review* Dec1956 **cover misprint**
Edward Winter has documented that December 1956 *Chess Review* front-cover promotion `Game of the Century (See page 370)` has a **false pointer**: page 370 actually carries Kmoch's annotations of **Smyslov–Pachman**, whereas the Byrne–Fischer `Game of the Century` occurs on printed **pages 374–375**, already visually verified from US Chess-supplied copy.

Historian secondary textual source: https://www.chesshistory.com/winter/extra/byrnefischer.html
**Rule for Engine source registries**: `advertised_page` ≠ `actual_article_pages`; record both and a `pagination_discrepancy`. Never import the front-cover pointer as location truth.

### 1958–1959 *Chess World* criticism/rebuttal chain
Winter separately identifies a later Australian literature exchange:
- `Chess World`, September 1958 **pp.203–204** reproduced Kmoch's Byrne–Fischer notes.
- `Chess World`, November 1958 **p.257**, F.A. Crowl disputed Kmoch on **move 33**.

## B. Polish 1942 comparator — **printed page identified**
Historian Edward Winter credits Henryk Konaszczuk with identifying the **Russner–Walcker** game on **printed page 3** of `Goniec Krakowski`, cited as **22 November 1942**. He separately gives the *actual game event* as **28 June 1942**, Warsaw, Warsaw–Cracow match, and players **H.W. Russner** and **K. Walcker**. He warns earlier variant names **Rüssher/Walcicer** are erroneous:
https://www.chesshistory.com/winter/extra/byrnefischer.html

**Date caution**: institution IPN supplied digital catalogue issue **#274, 22/23 November 1942**, exact JBC edition https://www.jbc.bj.uj.edu.pl/dlibra/publication/24391/edition/18938. Winter also identifies **a different game on the same printed column**, **Efim Bogoljubow vs K. Walcker**, Kraków 14 April 1942; do not accidentally merge its chess notation into the Russner–Walcker comparator. The Polish wartime publication requires contextual framing, as occupation-era press.

## F. Additional primary-bibliography conflict around Walcker (catalogue-level)
Edward Winter's Chess Notes `C.N.7930` transmits researcher Peter Anderberg's inspection of `Deutsche Schachblätter` **1 April 1941 pp.60–61** and **1 Jan 1942 p.11**: German magazine p.61 identifies Walcker's forename as **Carl**, and its contemporaneous results differ from the historical 1942 Goniec account of the **1941 Cracow championship**. The German report says Walcker won with 7.5/9 ahead of Mross; the 1942 German account says Mross subsequently won with seven wins and Walcker equal third (4.5 points). The Polish Goniec issue is cited by Winter as claiming first in 1940 and second in 1941. These are **historian-quoted magazine accounts**, not three original source pages we visually inspected. Record as `NAME_VARIANT_CARL_K_WALCKER` and `YEAR_AND_RESULTS_DISPUTED_BY_DIFFERENT_PERIODICALS`; do NOT silently reconcile which event/year each record describes.

## H. 1956 hand-written image misidentification reiterated
Modern ChessBase and an official FIDE social media image share a well-known autograph sheet and label it `original`; Donaldson's 2025 publisher source asserts a widely reproduced autograph sheet is **Fischer's later postgame reconstruction**. A social-media publisher caption or print reproduction is not sufficient evidence to link a pictured autograph to the original object. Avoid reproducing these copyrighted/uncleared images or declaring a conclusive match from low-resolution crops.
