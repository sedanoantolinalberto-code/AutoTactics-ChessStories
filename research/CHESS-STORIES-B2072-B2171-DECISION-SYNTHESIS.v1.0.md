## Material new finding: holdings trail for the 1942 newspaper

The Polish Academy of Sciences' **Kórnik Library** catalog describes *Goniec Krakowski*, shelfmark **Cz 4490**, with selected surviving issues from 1942 and a clear warning about gaps in the holdings. The listing includes numbers 263–266, 284, 288 and 297, among many others, but this does not establish the availability of the exact issue for **22 November 1942**, nor the presence or identity of its page 3.

Source: https://platforma.bk.pan.pl/pl/search_results/470801

A **29 November 1942, No. 280** digital PDF is indexed at a Polish digital-library host, with chess-column material in its text representation. This proves neither that 22 November was digitized there nor that its issue number can be safely inferred by subtraction. No claimed visual examination of that PDF is made in this tranche.

Source: https://jbc-test.dlibra.psnc.pl/Content/18944/PDF/DIGCZAS001749_1942_280.pdf

Edward Winter reports that **Henryk Konaszczuk** located the **22 November 1942 page 3** containing the game in *Goniec Krakowski*, attributing the match to **Warsaw, 28 June 1942**, and identifying players **H.W. Russner** and **K. Walcker**, whereas prior secondary text also used H.R. Russner / Carl Walcker. This remains Winter's reported source observation, **not direct visual inspection by the Chess Stories project**.

Source: https://www.chesshistory.com/winter/extra/byrnefischer.html

## Provenance, history and ethical context

The Kórnik catalog identifies the newspaper as a Polish-language occupation-era newspaper published under German occupation, with propaganda context. It must be described as a historical primary *artifact* when accessed, **not** endorsed for its editorial ideology or presumed reliable for every claim. A literal move score, event date and player identification can each have different degrees of corroboration. The holding institution, digitizer, photographer and copyright holders may differ.

## Decision

**Highest expected evidence value:** locate the issue dated 22 November 1942 at the original digitization/catalogue level or request page 3 from a lawful custodian, then document precise issue number, edition, page and source identifiers. Do not guess issue number based on 29 November. An independent match witness and exact original move transcription are subsequent separate tasks.
