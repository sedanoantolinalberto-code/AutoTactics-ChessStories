## De-duplication / provenance decisions
- Two institutions cataloguing a periodical do not constitute independent testimony for a chess move.
- A catalogue record describing original printed holdings is distinct from an accessible scan or a page read.
- A photographic archive unit is distinct from tournament score sheets and cannot validate a complete game.
- If future reports overlap, record textual dependency rather than counting documents as independent event witnesses.
- Keep specific issue/page, object identity, original custody, download rights, media reuse and SAN legality in separate fields.
