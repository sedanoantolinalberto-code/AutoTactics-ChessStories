## Institutional procedural constraints
The same reply explains that individual IPN archival research requests under article 36(1)(2) of the 1998 IPN statute require a formal application. Non-academic applicants additionally need a recommendation from a qualified academic researcher in the relevant disciplines; scientific staff are exempt from that recommendation. It names the Warsaw IPN branch director as competent in the situation of an applicant with no past or current Polish residence.

IPN researcher form: https://eng.ipn.gov.pl/en/arch/8209,Application-forms-for-researchers.html

**Practical choice:** use the freely accessible JBC copy first if possible, without treating the formal IPN process as a prerequisite for public catalog access.

## Research and ethical context
The 1942 newspaper was a German-occupation-era Polish-language publication. In later historical treatments it is categorized among occupation-era propaganda newspapers; archival consultation must not be confused with endorsement of its non-chess content. Keep source attribution, provenance and framing for any future editorial use. The 1942 material is a **segregated comparator**, not evidence that Fischer copied or knew a position.
