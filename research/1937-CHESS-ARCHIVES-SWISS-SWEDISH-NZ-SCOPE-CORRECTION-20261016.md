## 3. New 1937 overseas republication chain
Original issue text published via New Zealand National Library Papers Past:
https://paperspast.natlib.govt.nz/newspapers/ESD19371211.2.24
`Evening Star`, 11 December 1937, chess column, explicitly identifies earlier sources:
- British monthly `Chess` October 1937 account **`Euwe and Alekhine at Grips Again`**
- `Manchester Guardian` two parallel player statements headed `A Handshake Before the Battle` (as the NZ columnist describes).
- Czech `Šachový týden` (historical ASCII rendering `Sachovy Tyden`) Alekhine interview as summarized by that journalist.

These are **claimed attribution links contained in the contemporary NZ article**, NOT original pages of the British/UK or Czech publications and not confirmed independent eyewitness observations. Newspaper’s personal-health anecdotes are attributed journalism, not established medical facts. The preceding newspaper chess problem/game notation belongs to other contests, not necessarily the Euwe–Alekhine first game.
