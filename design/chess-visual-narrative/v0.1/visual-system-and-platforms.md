# EP01 — clean-room visual language and platform strategy v0.1
**Design candidate, not approved implementation or measured audience result.** Source continuity: README.md and storyboard-T01-T12.md. Avoid a stylized chessboard that loses positions, and avoid merely reproducing a flat tutorial board.

## Creative thesis

**A micro-documentary about a decision point, not a move generator.** The visual language combines a very legible square-accurate board with restrained cinematic atmosphere: tension from framing and edit rhythm, clarity from truth labels and highlighted destinations. A dramatic move is still an ordinary chess event unless the sources justify a stronger claim. Do not represent actual player faces, archival camera recordings or physical period rooms as genuine historic evidence.

### Two-plane proposal

**Truth plane (locked to data).** Board occupancy and perspective remain legible; active semantic branch, labels and move coordinates are not affected by camera flourish. Every pictured position must be a declared VISUAL state. All overlays remain visible for their required factuality window. Never infer legal paths, evaluations or moves from interpolated graphics.

**Atmosphere plane (free to design, subject to rights).** Subtle background grain, depth-of-field outside the chessboard, edge lighting on brand-new geometric pieces, micro-parallax of nonchess layers, camera pushes that preserve labels and all necessary squares. These are entirely original-design *intentions*, not commercial-rights-approved assets.

### Proposed visual hierarchy for a vertical 9:16 program

1. Factuality label and branch status in a **persistent high-contrast** top/side region; always uncut and readable. Exact Spanish labels must be preserved.
2. Board containing **all 64 squares** with visible origin/destination and player-to-move orientation; no crop that erases a relevant square. Data source = declared VISUAL state only.
3. Clear micro-caption for event notation and one concrete consequence; never obscure squares or pieces.
4. Lightweight move timeline with Txx scene progress, branch marker and a visible editorial skip for T10. This is NOT a fabricated historical score tracker.
5. Optional background atmosphere and end-card, kept outside safety and gameplay regions.

There is no fixed pixel safe-area in this RFC: mobile app overlays and UI placements change. Future layouts must be checked on real 9:16 interfaces for YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels and TikTok individually, in addition to a neutral full-frame reference. A 1080×1920 master is a **format proposal**, not a rendered artifact or verified platform requirement. A 16:9 alternative may relocate contextual commentary to a sidebar, but the underlying T01–T12 truth/position sequence cannot change.

### Typography and color — proposals, not licences

- **Text:** bold grotesk family candidate (Noto Sans remains preferred from baseline but no font binary/version/license record selected). Alternative system fonts can be explored only under separate licensing review.
- **REAL:** restrained graphite and warm neutral framing; **HYPOTHETICAL:** physically distinct dashed border or split-screen texture **plus exact words**; **REWIND:** timeline chevrons plus explicit label; **ELLIPSIS:** bracketed gap with 14 plies count. No semantic information solely by hue.
- **Board pieces:** project-original vector source candidates; no reuse of external icons, board screenshots, FIDE/Chess.com/lichess branding or third-party silhouettes without clearance. Explicit binary and rights provenance still needed.
- **Contrast:** design for high-text clarity even after mobile compression. Future accessibility must assess magnification, color-vision deficiencies, silent comprehension and rapid on-screen disclosures, without implying a current score.

### Camera and motion grammar

| Scene purpose | Candidate treatment | Forbidden shortcut |
|---|---|---|
| Establish geometry | Slow outward reveal of square-accurate board at V-P1 | Board tilt/crop hiding critical squares |
| Show move | Single board-anchored trace g4→e6 or c5→b6 for declared REAL_MOVE | Extra pieces, invented intermediate states or best-move arrows |
| Show capture | Target-square focus, piece removed as part of declared move | Showing a separate capture that would add a ply |
| Rewind | Timeline scrub/graphic jump directly V-REAL→V-P1 | Reverse chess-move animation interpreted as an event |
| Alternative | Distinct dashed narrative frame, persistent 'HIPOTÉTICA · NO JUGADA' | Yellow/blue-only warning or unlabelled 17...Qd8 |
| Restore truth | Strictly restore MAIN+V-P1 while retaining hypothetical disclosure until complete | Fade/remove label while V-HYP is still visible |
| Editorial gap | Hold V-REAL + an explicit 36–49 gap | Animating fourteen unspecified board positions |
| After-only capture | Hard cut to V-EV2 with d1 annotation | Revealing V-PRE-EV2, moving knight directly from V-REAL |
| Close | Static V-EV2 + 'CIERRE EDITORIAL · LA PARTIDA CONTINÚA' | 'Final', 'checkmate', final score or a fictional final winning pose |

Board lighting is *not* a chess input; screen animations cannot establish legality, absolute time, historical certainty, consent or asset clearance.

## Silent-first release research architecture (future only)

A silent story treatment is a **conceptual rights minimization choice**, not permission to render, deploy or show humans. Audio-free captions and board must express: (a) REAL, (b) hypothetical *before and during* 17...Qd8, (c) real restoration, (d) T10 gap, (e) T12 nonterminal conclusion.

A later voiced edition is a **separate derived project** with separate voice/TTS and music/SFX licensing checks; no existing or proposed audio belongs to the silent candidate. Naming any speech voice or music here would not license it.

## Platform variants — same canonical twelve-event spine

| Proposed destination | Content framing candidate | Invariants, not negotiable |
|---|---|---|
| **YouTube Shorts 9:16** | Hook through queen capture; explanatory rewind in middle; T12 as nonterminal final caption | All 12 events remain causally distinguishable; required labels cannot be cropped/covered by player UI |
| **Instagram Reels 9:16** | Cover/first visual frame highlights 'real versus hypothetical' and the b6 capture; no account UI mockups presented as verified | Same move identities, alternative status, omission and ending |
| **TikTok 9:16** | Captions prioritised for sound-off consumption and brief visual attention; large textual claim rather than fast tiny arrows | No loss of hypothetical warning; exact restore and no invented interstitial move |
| **Longer 16:9 derivative** | More contextual board annotations; potentially one side panel for source qualification and definitions | Does not add 1942 comparison as established proof or add terminal 1956 score |

All three vertical variants should originate from **one narrative truth plan**, not from different independently authored chess histories. They are candidate editorial derivatives; no algorithmic performance or engagement uplift is claimed.

### Runtime variants and density

The existing R6 baseline discusses **45-second and 60-second *editorial targets***. Actual output lengths, shot starts and durations are **UNMEASURED**. Any future condensed target must shrink holds/words/ornamentation rather than delete T05 warning, T07 restore, T10 explicit gap, T11 technical-only protection or T12 historical nonterminal close. No number here is an output measurement or execution budget.

## Design boundaries for future production

- Do not import original or derivative chess publication scans; permission for media reproduction remains NOT_AUTHORIZED.
- Do not treat a screenshot of a proposed font as approval of binary licensing.
- Do not simulate historic chess clock digits, a recorded player emotion, viewer reactions or true original dates beyond source declarations.
- The source-declared positions and moves require qualified semantic/oracle review before production; a visually convincing mockup is not a move-proof.
- No visual QA, mobile-device check, design user study, silent comprehension test or copyright originality review has occurred.
- R6 PENDING, IVV_GLOBAL/CASE_APPROVAL NOT APPROVED, E5B4D NO-GO under M and strict Policy B remain binding.
