## Audience proposition

Chess Stories should answer two different questions without confusing their truth status:

1. **What is declared to happen in the real sequence?** 17...Be6, 18.Bxb6 and the later separately presented 25...Nxd1, with the precise omission.
2. **What is an editorial counterfactual?** 17...Qd8 is labeled **HIPOTÉTICA · NO JUGADA** before and during any visual treatment.

The content hook is decision-point clarity, *not* a claim that a historical player calculated, intentionally sacrificed material, copied a 1942 game or delivered verified mate. A reader must be able to identify what is sourced, editorial and still unverified.

### Cover-card concepts (text only, no illustration generated)

1. **«REAL / HIPOTÉTICA»** with one original board image after future qualified implementation, never two different candidates presented as historical screenshots.
2. **«17...Be6 / ¿17...Qd8?»** with ‘¿’ and 'NO JUGADA' to distinguish the counterfactual.
3. **«LA PARTIDA CONTINÚA»** framing nonterminal narrative; never 'jaque mate en 1956' as the endpoint of this clip.

Any eventual cover artwork must be new, exact-byte tracked and independently rights-reviewed. No named player portrait, game magazine fragment or tournament badge is automatically available.

Do not suggest access to unseen original Chess Review pages.
- **Premium possibility (future, not authorized):** annotated decision diagrams, longer comparative story, source-method notes, original illustrations and a research reading list; must not trade on unverified claims or reprint pages/art without rights.
- **List ethics / compliance:** consent-based signup, clear purpose, easy unsubscribe, privacy-compliant tooling and handling before any signup is deployed; no fake subscriber testimonials or paywall of others' copyrighted archives.
- **Separation:** newsletters and product descriptions must preserve claim-assessment status instead of silently upgrading `UNASSESSED` claims to facts.
