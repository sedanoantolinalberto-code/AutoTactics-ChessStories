## 3. Secondary score comparison

Edward Winter's article (URL above) publishes a full Byrne–Fischer game score with the focal:
- 17.Kf1 followed by **17...Be6**
- **18.Bxb6**
- **24...Ra4** as a move that was actually played, NOT as a documented unique optimum
- **25...Nxd1**, after which play continues with **26.h3**
- Final **41...Rc2 mate**

The names, order and positions of these selected moves corroborate the supplied R2 *secondary candidate* and the story's distinction between the editorial cut at ply 50 and final game score at ply 82.

**Independence caveat:** Secondary agreement does not prove that the historian and the preserved R2 PGN rely on fully independent contemporaneous witnesses. The game-score text here was retrieved from a modern historiographical article, not newly transcribed by us from the 1956 magazine or Fischer's original handwritten score sheets. No historical acceptance gate is promoted.

**Additional accessible modern parallel:** ChessBase's retrospective
https://en.chessbase.com/post/the-game-that-shook-the-world-2
describes the final mate and 0–1 and reproduces a photograph of a scoresheet described as Fischer's 1956 manuscript. We have not authenticated that underlying manuscript or obtained image-republication permission.

**Date claim:** Winter identifies October 17, 1956 from independent secondary book/scoresheet references, and notes an inconsistency in Fischer's later book introduction about the tournament month range. Avoid upgrading a precise original-record date/venue claim until cited original records are inspected and documented.

**Significant documentary advance, but not a completed 82-ply transcription.**

- **Original publication:** *Chess Life*, Vol. XI, No. 5, Monday **5 November 1956** (printed on first page).
- **Archive provenance:** US Chess historical digital archive, https://uscf1-nyc1.aodhosting.com/CL-AND-CR-ALL/CL-ALL/1956/1956_11_1.pdf
- **PDF locator:** **PDF page index 1 / printed page 2**, under "GRUENFELD DEFENSE", "Rosenwald Trophy Tournament", New York, 1956, "White D. Byrne / Black R. Fischer" (name scan/OCR garbled in places).
- **Game record format:** 41 numbered full moves in **English descriptive chess notation** and a declared final **mate** on move 41, rather than modern SAN.
- **Observed retrieval evidence:** indexed extraction of the publisher-archived eight-page PDF includes the game table on PDF page 2, approximately text lines 445–471 in its full text response. The article is not merely a modern paraphrase.
