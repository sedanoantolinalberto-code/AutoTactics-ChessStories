## Source-grounded license clarification

**DejaVu fonts are not automatically governed by the SIL Open Font License.**

The DejaVu project publishes its own composite license:
- https://dejavu-fonts.github.io/License.html
- https://github.com/dejavu-fonts/dejavu-fonts/blob/master/LICENSE

That license includes the Bitstream Vera grant and its obligations, plus attribution/conditions for glyphs derived from Arev in some fonts. The license permits broad use and redistribution subject to specific conditions; it also imposes name-change rules on modified fonts and retains required notices for font-software redistribution. This general permissive source text is **not** a complete license review of the specific font binary embedded in the old v4 stills. Without a binary file hash, version, glyph origin and distribution mode, no exact-asset commercial clearance may be asserted.

Do not bundle fonts in GitHub, public eBooks, videos or downloadable source archives before verifying whether font software itself is being distributed, whether embedded glyphs carry separate requirements and whether notices are required. Rendering ordinary text may have different rights considerations from redistributing the font file; the needed answer depends on the actual intended use.

**US Chess digital magazine archive is research-accessible but not a commercial-rights blanket grant.**

Official source: https://new.uschess.org/chess-life-digital-archives

The archive states that PDFs are provided for **personal and research use only**, directing users wishing to go beyond U.S. copyright fair use to seek permission. This is stronger evidence than a search snippet that a specific magazine scan can be shown in monetized Shorts. Do not commit or embed December 1956 cover scans, Hans Kmoch's article, historical chess-score-sheet photographs or third-party portraits without a separately documented permissions basis and usage scope.

The Byrne–Fischer move sequence and mathematical board positions are facts, but that does not by itself clear the creative annotations, scanned photographs, decorative art, font files, soundtrack, generated silhouettes or adapted commentary.

## Asset grouping and commercial release decision

**Actual source/byte verification is missing for most categories**. The JSON register enumerates five v4 visual board positions, a technical-only pre-capture FEN, chess-piece silhouettes, candidate DejaVu typeface, draft Spanish narration script, unselected TTS, unselected music/SFX, archived periodical source, historical scoresheet photo and uncreated final video.

Each used asset must have:
1. Immutable exact file identity and SHA-256.
2. Credible creator/rightsholder and derivation provenance.
3. License/permission basis for the *specific type of exploitation* (YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, TikTok, newsletter, print/digital eBook and potential commercial ads).
4. Required attribution, notices, geographic/time limitations, sublicensing restrictions and revocation conditions.
5. Independent signatory review and a separately recorded rights verdict.

A license on one *component* does not automatically clear the composite finished PNG or MP4. An original narration script does not authorize use of someone else's photograph or audio.
